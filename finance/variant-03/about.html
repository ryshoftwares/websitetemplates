Our story

Nearly four decades of steady, independent advice.

Northbridge Advisory was founded in Providence, Rhode Island in 1988. Today we work with more than 1,400 client households across New England, guided by the same idea our founder started with: advice should be explained plainly and free of conflicts.

Our mission

Advice you can understand, from people who stick around.

We're an independent Registered Investment Adviser, which means we have a legal duty to act in our clients' best interest. We don't sell proprietary funds or earn commissions on products we recommend — our only compensation is the advisory fee our clients agree to.

What guides us

Four principles behind every plan.

Fiduciary, always

We're legally obligated to put your interests ahead of our own on every recommendation.

Plain-language explanations

If we can't explain a recommendation simply, we haven't finished doing our job.

Long-term relationships

Many of our clients have worked with the same advisor for fifteen years or more.

Measured, not reactive

We build plans that hold up across market cycles rather than chasing short-term headlines.

Milestones

Key moments in our history.

1988 — Founded in Providence

Harold Chase opened Northbridge Advisory's first office, working from a single room with a handful of client families.

1996 — Became an independent RIA

Registered as an independent investment adviser to remove commission-based conflicts from our advice.

2005 — Retirement planning practice expanded

Built a dedicated team focused on retirement income modeling and Social Security strategy.

2016 — Reached 1,000 client households

Crossed a milestone built almost entirely on referrals from clients, attorneys and accountants.

2024 — Surpassed $620M in assets under management

Continued measured growth while keeping the advisor-to-client ratio the same as it's always been.
