Our story

Founded on the idea that advice should outlast any single market cycle.

Meridian Wealth was founded in Boston in 1994 with a handful of client families. Thirty-one years later, we're an independent advisory practice serving families and business owners across the country from two offices.

Our mission

Advice that puts your plan ahead of any single product.

We believe good financial advice is a relationship, not a transaction. Every client works directly with a senior advisor who understands their full picture — income, family, business interests and long-term goals — rather than a rotating cast of representatives. We're independently owned, which means our recommendations aren't tied to any proprietary fund family or product quota.

What guides us

Four principles behind every recommendation.

Independence first

We're not owned by a bank or insurer, so our guidance isn't shaped by a parent company's targets.

Plain-language advice

We explain the reasoning behind every recommendation in terms you can repeat to your family.

Continuity over turnover

The advisor who onboards you is the advisor who answers your calls five, ten, twenty years later.

Risk-aware by default

We build plans around what could go wrong, not just what we hope will go right.

Milestones

Three decades of measured growth.

1994 — Founded in Boston

Robert Ashcombe opened Meridian Wealth's first office with a small group of client families referred by his early career in trust administration.

2003 — Trust & Estate Advisory launched

Built a dedicated practice to coordinate estate strategy directly with clients' attorneys and accountants.

2012 — Opened the Greenwich, CT office

Expanded to serve a growing base of tri-state area families and business-owner clients.

2020 — Family Office Services introduced

Formalized bill-pay, philanthropic and multi-generational coordination services for larger client families.

2025 — Surpassed $3.8B in assets under advisement

Crossed a milestone built on decades of referrals rather than advertising.
