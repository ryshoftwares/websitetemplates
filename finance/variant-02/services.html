Services

One platform. Every rail your finance team needs.

Most customers start with business banking and layer on treasury, cards and payouts as they scale. Everything lives in one dashboard and one login.

Business Banking

FDIC-insured checking and savings accounts with unlimited free sub-accounts, so every project, entity or department gets its own ledger without opening a new bank relationship.

  • Free sub-accounts
  • No minimum balance
  • Same-day ACH
  • Multi-entity support

Treasury & Cash Management

Automated cash flow forecasting, sweep accounts and yield on idle balances — with full visibility into where every dollar sits, updated in real time.

  • Cash flow forecasting
  • Automated sweeps
  • Yield on idle cash
  • Real-time reporting

Corporate Cards & Expense Management

Physical and virtual cards with real-time spend controls, automatic receipt matching, and approval workflows your team won't fight against.

  • Virtual & physical cards
  • Spend controls
  • Receipt capture
  • Approval workflows

Payments & Payouts API

ACH, wire and instant payout infrastructure for platforms that need to move money on behalf of their own customers — with compliance and monitoring built in.

  • ACH & wire
  • Instant payouts
  • Developer APIs
  • Built-in compliance
