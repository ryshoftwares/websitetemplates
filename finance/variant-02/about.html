Our story

Built by people who were done waiting on banking.

Ledger Labs started in 2019 when two payments engineers got fed up watching startups lose days to wire delays and clunky business banking portals. Today we process billions in payment volume for over 12,000 companies.

Our mission

Financial infrastructure that keeps up with how fast you actually move.

We think banking should feel like software: fast, transparent and built for the way modern finance teams actually work. That means real APIs, real-time visibility into every dollar, and a support team that answers in minutes, not days.

What we believe

Four ideas behind everything we build.

Speed is a feature

Instant account opening, same-day support and payouts that clear in minutes, not days.

Transparent pricing

No hidden fees, no minimum balance traps, no "call us for pricing" games.

Compliance without friction

Bank-grade compliance built into the product, not bolted on as a slow manual process.

Built with our customers

Our roadmap is shaped by finance teams who tell us what's actually broken in their week.

Milestones

Seven years of shipping fast.

2019 — Founded in Austin

Two payments engineers left their jobs to build the business banking product they wished existed.

2020 — Waitlist opens

Over 500 companies signed up for early access in the first month, entirely by word of mouth.

2021 — Seed round & corporate cards

Closed a $9M seed round and launched corporate cards with real-time spend controls.

2023 — 10,000 businesses

Crossed 10,000 business customers and launched our instant payouts API for platforms.

2025 — $9.4B in annual volume

Processed more payment volume in a single year than our first five years combined.
