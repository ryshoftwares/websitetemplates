Our story

We built the bootcamp we wished existed.

LEARNLAB started in 2016 in a shared co-working space in Austin, founded by three engineers who kept meeting brilliant self-taught developers that no one would interview. We built a program designed around what hiring managers actually test for.

Our mission

Skills-first education, measured in job offers.

We don't measure success by completion certificates. We measure it by interviews scheduled, offers signed and salaries negotiated — which is why every curriculum decision starts with a conversation with our employer partners, not a textbook.

Milestones

Ten years of building louder, faster.

2016

Founded in Austin

Three engineers launch LEARNLAB's first cohort of 18 students out of a shared co-working space, teaching full-stack JavaScript nights and weekends.

2018

First placement guarantee

LEARNLAB becomes one of the first bootcamps to offer a tuition refund if a graduate isn't hired within six months of finishing.

2020

Fully remote, fully global

Cohorts go fully online, opening enrollment to students outside Austin for the first time and tripling annual enrollment.

2022

200+ employer partners

The employer partner network passes 200 companies, giving every capstone project a live audience of real hiring managers.

2025

AI-augmented curriculum

Every program adds a dedicated unit on working with AI coding and design tools, reflecting how our hiring partners actually build today.

What drives us

Four rules every instructor follows.

Teach what's hireable

If employer partners aren't asking for a skill this year, it doesn't make the syllabus.

Show your work

Every unit ends in a shipped project, not a multiple-choice quiz.

Coach, don't just teach

Instructors run office hours, review resumes and make warm intros — the job is more than lecturing.

Publish the numbers

Our placement rate and starting salaries are public and updated every cohort — no cherry-picking.
