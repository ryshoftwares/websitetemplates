Academic programs

Eight degrees. One small, discussion-based campus.

Every Oakridge program combines a core liberal arts foundation with focused major coursework, culminating in a senior thesis or capstone project. Filter by area of study below.

Arts & Humanities

Bachelor of Arts in History

A seminar-based major covering ancient through modern history, with a required senior thesis built from primary-source archival research.

4-year programUndergraduate
Arts & Humanities

Bachelor of Arts in English Literature

Close reading, critical theory and creative writing workshops, taught entirely in discussion sections of sixteen students or fewer.

4-year programUndergraduate
Sciences

Bachelor of Science in Computer Science

A rigorous core in algorithms, systems and software engineering, with a required capstone built alongside a regional employer partner.

4-year programUndergraduate
Sciences

Bachelor of Science in Environmental Studies

Combines field ecology, policy and data analysis, with a summer field research term at the Institute's forest preserve station.

4-year programUndergraduate
Business

Bachelor of Business Administration

Core coursework in finance, operations and management, paired with a required semester-long consulting practicum for a local business.

4-year programUndergraduate
Business

Bachelor of Arts in Economics

4-year programUndergraduate
