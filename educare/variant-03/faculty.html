Faculty

Professors who teach, not just publish.

Every Oakridge professor teaches undergraduate sections themselves — there are no teaching assistants standing between students and the faculty who lead each department.

Dr. Eleanor Voss

Professor of History, Department Chair

Ph.D. in History, Yale University. Teaches early American history and directs the senior thesis program.

Dr. Samuel Okonkwo

Professor of Computer Science

Ph.D. in Computer Science, Carnegie Mellon University. Leads the Ferris Science Building's systems and software research group.

Dr. Helena Marsh

Professor of Economics

Ph.D. in Economics, University of Chicago. Teaches econometrics and advises the Economics honors track.

Dr. Nathaniel Cole

Professor of Environmental Studies

Ph.D. in Environmental Science, University of California, Berkeley. Directs the Institute's forest preserve field station.

Dr. Adaeze Nwosu

Professor of Education, Director of Graduate Studies

Ed.D., Harvard Graduate School of Education. Leads the Master of Education program for working teachers.

Dr. Julian Ferris

Professor of English Literature

Ph.D. in English, Oxford University. Teaches Victorian literature and runs the Institute's creative writing workshop series.
