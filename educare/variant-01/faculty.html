Faculty & leadership

The people who make small classes work.

Our department chairs teach every day — they're not administrators who left the classroom. Meet the six educators guiding Bright Path's academic program.

Dr. Miriam Kessler

Head of School

Ed.D. in Educational Leadership, Columbia University. Leading Bright Path since 2015, with 22 years in independent school administration.

Thomas Adeyemi

Dean of STEM & Innovation

M.S. in Mechanical Engineering, MIT. Built and coaches the school's award-winning robotics team.

Claire Duvall

Director of Visual & Performing Arts

M.F.A. in Fine Arts, Rhode Island School of Design. Oversees the Aldridge Arts Center and its exhibition and performance calendar.

Dr. Owen Whitfield

Chair, Humanities Department

Ph.D. in English Literature, University of Virginia. Teaches AP Literature and advises the student literary magazine.

Priya Nathan

Director of World Languages

M.A. in Applied Linguistics, Middlebury College. Built Bright Path's Spanish immersion track and its Oaxaca exchange partnership.

Marcus Bell

Director of Athletics & Wellness

M.Ed. in Sport Pedagogy, University of North Carolina. Oversees fourteen varsity teams and the wellness seminar curriculum.
