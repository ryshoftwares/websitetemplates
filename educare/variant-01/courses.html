Academics

A curriculum built for students who like to go deep.

Every Upper School student builds a four-year course plan with their advisor, mixing honors, Advanced Placement and IB Diploma coursework across five departments. Explore a sample of our course catalog below, or filter by subject area.

STEM

AP Biology & Field Research

A lab-heavy year of molecular and organismal biology, capped by an independent field research project at the Ashford Hills wetlands preserve.

Grades 11–12Full year · AP
STEM

Computer Science & Robotics

An introduction to Python and physical computing that culminates in a team-built robot entered in the regional robotics competition.

Grades 9–10Semester · Intro
STEM

AP Calculus BC

The full BC sequence in a single year for students who've completed Calculus AB — limits, series and multivariable applications with weekly problem sets.

Grade 12Full year · AP
Humanities

American Literature & Rhetoric

A seminar-style survey of American voices from Whitman to Morrison, paired with a formal rhetoric unit and a spring debate tournament.

Grade 11Full year · Honors
Humanities

World History: Power & Revolution

A thematic look at the revolutions and empires that shaped the modern world, with a document-based research paper each term.

Grade 10Full year
Arts

Studio Art & Design Portfolio

A self-directed studio course for students building a college application portfolio, with weekly critiques and a spring gallery show.

Grades 9–12Full year · Portfolio
Arts

Chamber Orchestra & Music Theory

Weekly rehearsal and performance with the school's chamber ensemble, paired with a foundational music theory and composition unit.

Grades 7–12Full year

Spanish Immersion III
