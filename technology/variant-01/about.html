Our story

Founded by engineers who were tired of shipping mediocre software.

NovaTech started in a two-desk office in San Francisco in 2016. Ten years later we're a 42-person studio helping founders and enterprise product teams build things worth using.

Our mission

Software that respects the people who use it.

We believe good software is a craft, not a checklist. Every engagement pairs senior engineers and designers directly with your team — no account managers relaying messages, no junior-heavy staffing. We ship in small, visible increments so you always know what's coming next.

What we value

Four principles that shape every engagement.

Craft over speed

We move fast, but never at the expense of code that has to live for years.

Transparent by default

Shared boards, weekly demos, honest timelines — you see exactly what's happening.

Senior on every team

No engagement runs without an engineer who has shipped this kind of system before.

Outcomes, not hours

We're accountable to the business result, not the number of tickets closed.

Milestones

Nine years of building.

2016 — Founded in San Francisco

Started with three engineers and one client: a healthcare startup that needed its first real platform.

2019 — Opened the Austin studio

Grew past 20 people and opened a second studio to support clients across time zones.

2022 — Launched our AI & Data practice

Built a dedicated team focused on applied machine learning and data platform work.

2025 — 120+ products shipped

Crossed 120 launched products across fintech, healthtech and B2B SaaS.
