About CoreDigital

We'd rather solve the real problem than sell the bigger package.

Founded in Boston in 2014, CoreDigital has grown by doing unglamorous work well: right-sized infrastructure, clean data, and internal tools that people actually use.

Our approach

Right-sized, not oversized.

We've turned down projects that would have meant selling a client more infrastructure than they needed. Our recommendations are built around your actual usage and growth plan — not a standard package.

What guides us

Documentation
