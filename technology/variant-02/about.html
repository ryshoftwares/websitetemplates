Let's build

Our story

We started Flux to stop making the safe choice.

FluxLabs began in 2018 when three designers and an engineer got tired of pitching "the bold version" and shipping the safe one. Today we're 35 people who ship the bold version instead.

How we think

Loud ideas, tested rigorously.

Bold doesn't mean reckless. Every experimental interface we ship is prototyped, tested with real users and measured against a real metric before it goes live. The goal isn't to be different for its own sake — it's to be different in a way that works.

What we stand for

Four rules we don't break.

No template thinking

If it looks like every other SaaS site, we haven't done our job yet.

Prototype before you decide

Bold ideas get tested in days, not debated in meetings for months.

Motion means something

Every animation earns its place by clarifying, not decorating.

Ship, then sharpen

We'd rather learn from something real than polish something theoretical.
