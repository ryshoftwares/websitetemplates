Features

Everything Orbit does to help a habit stick.

Six features built around one idea: consistency beats perfection.

Gentle Streaks

Miss a day and your streak dims instead of resetting to zero — Orbit rewards showing up most of the time, not every single time.

  • Forgiving streak logic
  • Streak freeze days

Weekly Reviews

Every Sunday, Orbit shows you a simple recap: what you kept up with, what slipped, and one small suggestion for the week ahead.

  • No guilt-trip language
  • One suggestion, not ten

Adaptive Reminders

Reminders shift based on when you actually complete habits, not a fixed clock time you set once and forgot about.

  • Learns your patterns
  • Snooze without penalty

Home-Screen Widgets

Check today's progress without opening the app — widgets update live as you check habits off.

  • iOS & Android widgets
  • Lock-screen support

Trend Charts

Simple line and bar views of your consistency over months, not just a single week — see the actual trend, not just today.

  • 90-day trend view
  • Export to CSV

Shared Habits

Invite a partner or friend to a shared habit and see each other's streaks — accountability without a leaderboard.

  • Up to 3 shared habits
  • Private by default
