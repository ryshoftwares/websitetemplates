Our story

We started PULSE in a parking garage.

Founded in Miami in 2014 with one unlicensed warehouse party, PULSE now produces festivals and tours across 18 cities — still run by the same crew that threw that first night.

How we build a night

Sound, light and crowd flow, designed together.

Most promoters book an artist and hope the room does the rest. We engineer the room first — sightlines, sound coverage, and how 3,000 people move through a space — then book the lineup that fits it.

Milestones

Twelve years, still loud.

2014

One unlicensed warehouse party in Miami — 200 people, one DJ, no permit.

2018

Launched our first multi-day festival, drawing 8,000 fans over two nights.

2022

Took our first full concert tour on the road across 12 cities.

2026

120,000+ fans across festivals, club nights and tours this year alone.
