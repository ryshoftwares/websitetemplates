Our story

We started Lumen to stop treating production as an afterthought.

Founded in New York in 2012, Lumen began as a two-person film crew and grew into a full production studio spanning film, live events and brand experiences — still run by people who show up on set.

Our approach

One accountable crew, start to finish.

We don't hand a project off between departments. The team that develops your concept is the same team on set, in the edit bay, and at the load-out. That continuity is why clients come back for their second and third productions.

Milestones

Fourteen years of productions.

2012 — Founded in New York

Started as a two-person documentary crew shooting branded short films for local clients.

2016 — First feature film

Produced our first feature-length documentary, which screened at three regional festivals.

2020 — Launched our Live Events division

Expanded into immersive installations and concert production as demand for live work grew.

2026 — 180+ productions delivered

Now a 40-person studio spanning New York and Los Angeles.
