Services & departments

Eight departments. One shared record. One connected team.

Whatever brings you in, our departments work from the same chart — so your care stays coordinated from your first visit to your last.

Primary Care

Annual wellness visits, everyday illnesses, chronic condition check-ins and preventive screenings with a doctor who follows your health over time rather than treating one visit at a time.

Pediatrics

Well-child visits, immunization scheduling and same-day sick appointments in an exam room designed to feel less intimidating to younger patients.

Cardiology

Consultations, EKGs and echocardiograms, and ongoing management for blood pressure and heart rhythm concerns, coordinated directly with your primary doctor.

Women's Health

Routine gynecological exams, prenatal visits and family planning consultations in a private, unhurried setting with providers you can see visit after visit.

Behavioral Health

Confidential counseling and psychiatric consultations for anxiety, mood concerns and life transitions, available in person or by telehealth.

Telehealth Visits

Secure video appointments for follow-ups, prescription renewals and minor concerns, scheduled through the same booking process as an in-person visit.

Physical Therapy

Individualized rehabilitation plans for injury recovery, post-surgical support and ongoing mobility goals, built alongside your referring physician.

Diagnostic Imaging

On-site X-ray and ultrasound services with results shared directly to your chart, so your doctor can review them at your next visit without delay.

How a referral works

Moving between departments, without the runaround.

Talk to your doctor

Your primary care provider identifies the specialist or service that fits your situation.

We schedule it

Our front desk books the specialty appointment before you leave, whenever a slot allows.

Your chart travels with you

The specialist reviews your history in advance, so the visit starts with a conversation.

Your doctor gets the notes

Visit summaries flow back to your primary provider automatically, keeping everyone aligned.

Not sure which department is right for you?

Tell us what's going on in your appointment request, and we'll route you to the right provider.
