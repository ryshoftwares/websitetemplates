Our story

Built by clinicians who wanted patients to feel known, not processed.

Wellspring Health opened its doors in 1998 as a single family practice. Today we're a multi-specialty clinic with four locations across Ashcombe County — still guided by the same idea: care works better when your whole team is actually talking to each other.

Our approach

Care that fits around your life, not the other way around.

We keep our schedules realistic, our waiting rooms calm, and our records shared across every department you visit. Every provider on our team has access to the same chart, so you're never asked to repeat your history from scratch. Our goal is simple: make it easy to get the right care, from the right person, at the right time.

What we value

Four principles that guide every visit.

Time to listen

Our appointment slots are built so your provider isn't rushing to the next room before you've finished your questions.

One shared record

Every department — from primary care to cardiology — works from the same chart, so nothing gets lost between referrals.

Plain-language explanations

We explain what we're doing and why, in terms that make sense, before we ask you to decide anything.

Access when you need it

Same-week visits, telehealth options and weekend urgent hours at select locations keep care within reach.

Milestones

Nearly three decades of growing with this community.

1998 — Founded in downtown Ashcombe

Opened as a single family medicine practice with two physicians and a front desk of three.

2006 — Added pediatrics and women's health

Expanded into a true multi-specialty practice as our patients' families grew alongside us.

2014 — Opened our Cardiology & Behavioral Health center

Brought heart health and mental health care under the same roof as primary care for the first time.

2020 — Launched Wellspring Telehealth

Introduced video visits so patients could reach their care team without a trip to the clinic.

2025 — Four locations, one connected record

Reached four clinic locations across the county, all sharing a single coordinated patient record.
