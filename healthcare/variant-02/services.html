Services

Eight ways to get the care you actually need.

Every service below runs through the same VitalCare+ record, so switching between video and in-person care never means starting over.

Telehealth Video Visits

Book a face-to-face video appointment with a licensed provider, most available the same day, including evenings and weekends.

Urgent Virtual Care

For non-emergency concerns that can't wait, connect with the next available provider — typically within fifteen minutes.

Primary Care Membership

Ongoing care with one dedicated provider, plus unlimited secure messaging between scheduled visits.

Mental Health Therapy

Video sessions with a licensed therapist for anxiety, stress and life transitions, scheduled around your week.

Pediatric Telehealth

Video visits for kids' everyday illnesses and follow-ups, with a pediatric nurse practitioner available most evenings.

Chronic Condition Check-Ins

Regular virtual check-ins for ongoing conditions like blood pressure or diabetes, so small changes get caught early.

Prescription Management

Renewals and medication reviews handled by message or video visit, sent directly to your preferred pharmacy.

Lab Test Coordination

We order labs at a nearby partner facility and walk you through the results together on your next visit.
