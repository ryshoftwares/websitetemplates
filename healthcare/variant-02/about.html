Our story

We got tired of waiting rooms too.

VitalCare+ launched in 2019 with one goal: make it as easy to see a licensed provider as it is to order dinner. Six years later, we still measure ourselves by how quickly we can get you to the right person, virtually or in person.

Our mission

Fewer forms, faster answers, real people.

We built VitalCare+ around the idea that healthcare should adapt to your life, not the other way around. Every membership pairs you with a primary provider you'll see again, backed by a full team of specialists, therapists and nurses who share your care plan — so nothing gets lost between a video visit and an in-person one.

What we value

Four things every visit is built around.

Speed without rushing

Same-day access matters, but never at the cost of a provider actually listening to you.

One continuous record

Video visits, in-person appointments and messages all live in the same chart, for every provider you see.

Plain-language care

No jargon-heavy after-visit summaries — just clear next steps you can actually follow.

Privacy by design

Every visit and message is encrypted and handled under strict healthcare privacy standards.

Milestones

Six years of building a better front door to care.

2019 — Founded in Seattle

Launched with a handful of providers offering video visits to members across King County.

2021 — Added mental health therapy

Brought licensed therapists onto the platform after members told us they needed more than physical care.

2023 — Opened our Seattle care hub

Introduced in-person visits for the moments a video call isn't enough, tied to the same care record.

2025 — Expanded to Oregon and Idaho

Grew our licensed provider network to serve members across three states.
