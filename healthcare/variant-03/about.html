Our story

Started by a nurse who saw a gap in this neighborhood.

Cedar Clinic opened in 2003 as a two-room community health center. Over twenty years later, we're still independently run, still keep our fees on a sliding scale, and still believe steady, familiar care matters more than anything flashy.

Our approach

Care that stays consistent, visit after visit.

We keep our clinician team small and our patient panels realistic, so you tend to see the same doctor or nurse practitioner each time. Records are shared across every department we offer, and our front desk staff has, on average, worked here for over eight years — they often know your family before you've said a word.

What we value

Four things that haven't changed since 2003.

Familiarity over speed

We'd rather you see a provider who knows your history than get shuffled to whoever's free.

Fees that scale

Sliding-scale pricing is core to who we are, not an afterthought for a few patients.

Plain answers

We explain what's happening in your visit without rushing or talking over your head.

Staying independent

We've stayed a single, locally run clinic so decisions are made by people who work here.

Milestones

Twenty-three years, one neighborhood.

2003

Opened as a two-room community health center with one nurse practitioner and one exam table.

2009

Added family medicine and pediatrics as our patient base grew into a second generation of families.

2016

Introduced behavioral health counseling after patients told us it was the hardest care to find locally.

2021

Added telehealth check-ins so established patients could reach us without always coming in.

2024

Recognized as an NCQA Patient-Centered Medical Home for our coordinated approach to care.
