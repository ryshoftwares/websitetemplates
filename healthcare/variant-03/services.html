Services

Six services, all under one roof, all on one record.

Whatever brings your family in, our team shares one chart across every service listed below.

Family Medicine

Annual wellness visits, everyday illnesses and chronic condition follow-ups for every age in your household.

Pediatrics

Well-child visits, developmental checkups and immunization scheduling from infancy through adolescence.

Women's Health

Routine gynecological exams, prenatal visits and family planning consultations in a private, unhurried setting.

Behavioral Health

Confidential counseling for anxiety, stress and life transitions, available in person or by telehealth.

Chronic Disease Management

Ongoing support for conditions like diabetes and high blood pressure, with regular check-ins between visits.

Telehealth Check-Ins

Video follow-ups for established patients — medication reviews, minor concerns and check-ins that don't require a trip in.

Becoming a patient

A simple, four-step process.

Request a visit

Submit an appointment request online or call our front desk directly.

We confirm details

Our staff calls to confirm your appointment and check on sliding-scale eligibility if needed.

Your first visit

We build your chart and get to know your history so future visits move faster.

Ongoing care

You keep seeing the same clinician, with records shared across any service you use.
