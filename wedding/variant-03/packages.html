Questions

What couples usually ask first.

How far ahead should we book? +

Most couples book Full-Service or Partial Planning 12-16 months out, and Day-Of Coordination 2-3 months before their date.

Do you travel outside Boston? +

Yes — about a third of our weddings happen outside Massachusetts. Travel fees are quoted per location.

Can we upgrade packages later? +

Absolutely. Many couples start with Day-Of Coordination and add planning support once they see how much there is to track.
