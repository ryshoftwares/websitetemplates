Our story

We started this studio to take the noise out of wedding planning.

Founded in Boston in 2017 by lead planner Margit Hollis, The Wedding Studio exists for couples who want their wedding day to feel like them — without spending a year drowning in vendor spreadsheets to get there.

How we think about it

One planner. One plan. No surprises.

Every couple works with one lead planner from first call to last dance — not a rotating cast of coordinators. We believe the calmest weddings come from the clearest plans, so we'd rather over-communicate a timeline than improvise on the day.

Milestones

Nine years, one studio.

2017

Founded in Boston

Margit Hollis left a large event-planning firm to start a studio built around fewer weddings, done better.

2019

Added day-of coordination

Started supporting couples who'd planned everything themselves but needed a steady hand on the day.

2022

Grew to a three-planner studio

Brought on two associate planners, each trained in the same calm, checklist-driven approach.

2026

140+ weddings planned

Still capping each planner's calendar at a limited number of weddings a year, on purpose.
