Questions

What everyone asks before booking.

Do you only plan "nontraditional" weddings?

Nope — we plan weddings that are specific to the couple, whatever that means for you. Sometimes that's a warehouse rave, sometimes it's your grandmother's backyard with better lighting and no arguments about the seating chart. The common thread is that we start from what you actually want, not a template.

How far out do we need to book?

For The Whole Vibe or Full Send, 9–14 months out gets you the best pick of venues and vendors. The Elopement Remix can move much faster — we've pulled off a full elopement plan in three weeks when a couple needed to move quickly.

Do you work outside Portland and Austin?

Yes — we've planned weddings in 42 states. Travel fees apply outside the Pacific Northwest and Central Texas, quoted upfront with no surprises on the final invoice.

What's not included in the package price?

Vendor costs (venue, catering, photography, florals, music) are always separate and paid directly to those vendors — our price covers our planning, design and coordination time only. We'll help you build a realistic vendor budget in your first meeting.

Can we mix and match between packages?

Constantly. Most of our contracts start from one of these three tiers and get customized from there — swap in add-ons, drop something you don't need, or ask for something that isn't listed at all. Tell us the shape of your day and we'll quote it honestly.
