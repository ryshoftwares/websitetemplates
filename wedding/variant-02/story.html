Our story

We started Vow & Co. because seated dinners made us want to scream.

In 2016, planner Reyna Castillo walked out of her 200th identical ballroom wedding — same string quartet, same chicken-or-salmon card, same speech about "the journey" — and decided couples deserved a planner who'd actually ask what they wanted. Vow & Co. has been asking ever since.

How we think

Bold doesn't mean chaotic.

Every "wild idea" we greenlight — the taco truck instead of a plated dinner, the drag queen officiant, the ceremony that starts forty minutes late because the sunset schedule said so — gets a real timeline, a real budget line and a real backup plan. We don't do weird for the sake of weird. We do specific, and specific is what makes a wedding feel like the couple throwing it instead of a rental hall's idea of romance.

What we stand for

Four rules we don't break.

No template weddings

If your wedding could be swapped with the couple's before you at the same venue, we haven't done our job.

Say yes to weird

Punk cover band? Backyard taco bar? A ceremony officiated by your childhood dog-walker? We're in.

Your family, your rules

We run interference on the seating chart politics so you don't have to referee your own wedding.

Plan hard, party harder

Every spreadsheet exists so that, on the day, you're on the dance floor instead of checking one.

Milestones

Ten years, out loud.

2016

Reyna Castillo quit a traditional planning firm and founded Vow & Co. from her apartment, taking on one couple who wanted a punk-rock backyard wedding nobody else would touch.

2018

Booked our first full warehouse wedding in a decommissioned print shop. Word got around fast — by year's end we'd grown from a one-woman operation to a team of four.

2020

Every couple that year needed a plan B, so we built one: the Elopement Remix, a whole system for turning a 20-person backyard ceremony into something that still felt like a real wedding. It stuck around long after it had to.

2023

Crossed 250 weddings planned, brought design and styling fully in-house, and won "Best Non-Traditional Wedding Design" at the regional planners' awards.

2026

300+ weddings later, we're a crew of 14 planners, designers and coordinators working in 42 states — still turning down the seated dinner.
