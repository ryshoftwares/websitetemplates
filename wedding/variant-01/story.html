Our story

Built on one wedding, a card table, and a lot of stubbornness.

EverAfter began in 2015 when our founder planned her sister's wedding on a card table in a rented cottage, because no planner in the region would take a 90-day timeline. Eleven years later, we've planned over 120 weddings and built a studio around that same instinct: say yes to the couple, then figure out how.

♡

Our philosophy

Three beliefs behind every wedding we plan.

Your story, not a trend

We design around who you are as a couple — your families, your food, your favorite Sunday — never a Pinterest board that expires in a season.

Calm is the deliverable

A well-run wedding day looks effortless because someone did the hard, invisible work months in advance. That someone is us.

Craft over shortcuts

We work with a small, vetted circle of florists, chefs and photographers because craftsmanship compounds — and it shows on the day.

Milestones

Eleven years, one card table at a time.

2015

EverAfter is founded in Sonoma

Founder Camille Marchetti plans her sister's 90-day wedding on a card table, then opens EverAfter's doors three weeks later to her first paying couple.

2017

Our first published wedding

A 40-guest vineyard elopement we designed is featured in Style Me Pretty — the first of what would become forty-plus press placements.

2019

The team grows to five planners

We bring on our first associate planners and open a second studio location, allowing us to take on estate weddings across two coasts.

2022

Marlowe House partnership begins

EverAfter becomes the preferred planning studio for Marlowe House in Charleston, expanding our signature venue roster beyond California.

2025

Named Best Wedding Planner, Sonoma County

Readers of a regional bridal publication vote EverAfter Best Wedding Planner for the third year running, as our team surpasses 120 weddings planned.
