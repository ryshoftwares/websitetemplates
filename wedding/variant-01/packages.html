Frequently asked

Questions couples ask before booking.

How far in advance should we book?

Most Signature Day and Full Estate couples book 9–14 months out, since our calendar and our preferred venues fill quickly for peak spring and fall dates. Elopements can often be booked with as little as 60 days' notice.

Do we have to use your signature venues?

Not at all. The Hazel Barn and Marlowe House are venues we know intimately and love recommending, but all three packages can be planned at any venue you choose, anywhere in the continental U.S.

What's included in the planning fee versus vendor costs?

Package pricing covers our planning, design and coordination services only. Catering, florals, photography and venue rental are billed separately by those vendors — we build and manage that full budget with you from the first meeting.

Can we customize a package?

Yes. Every proposal is adjusted to your guest count, timeline and travel needs after an initial call — the tiers above are a starting point, not a fixed menu.

Not sure which package fits?

Send us your date and vision, and we'll recommend the right path in your first conversation with us.
