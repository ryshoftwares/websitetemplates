Signature venues

Two places we love to plan weddings.

Sonoma Valley, California

The Hazel Barn

A restored 1912 barn set among twelve acres of vineyard, olive groves and wildflower meadow. Guests move from a stone-terrace ceremony to a candlelit barn reception, with the option to tent the entire lawn for weather-proof celebrations of any size.

10–220guest capacity

Charleston, South Carolina

Marlowe House

An 1820s low-country estate with moss-draped oaks, a private dock and a columned veranda built for first looks. Marlowe House pairs Southern grandeur with an intimate, garden-party warmth — ideal for weekend-long estate weddings.
