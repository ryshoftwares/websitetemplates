Pricing, explained

How dispatch and pricing works.

What does the diagnostic fee cover?

It covers the technician's visit, inspection and a flat-rate repair quote. If you approve the repair on the spot, the diagnostic fee is credited toward the total.

Is the quote really flat-rate?

Yes — you approve one number before any work starts. If the job turns out to need more parts or labor than expected, we requote it and get your sign-off before continuing.

How fast can someone actually get here?

Average arrival for priority and emergency calls is under 90 minutes across the Austin metro. Scheduled jobs can be booked for a specific window up to two weeks out.
