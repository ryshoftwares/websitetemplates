Our story

We started as an app. We became the fastest crew in Austin.

FIXPOINT began in 2019 as a simple dispatch app matching homeowners with independent trade professionals — no call centers, no hold music.

How we started

Built by people tired of waiting for a plumber.

Co-founders Priya Shah and Marcus Doyle built the first version of FIXPOINT's dispatch app in 2019 after both had the same bad experience in the same month: booking a repair, then waiting four days for someone to show up. They figured Austin's independent electricians, plumbers and handymen didn't need fewer customers — they needed a better way to get matched with the right job, fast.

The app launched with eight technicians and a promise: real-time matching, flat-rate quotes, and a guaranteed response window. It worked. By 2022 FIXPOINT had onboarded its 1,000th technician to the network, and by 2025 the team had completed more than 12,000 jobs across Greater Austin.

FIXPOINT today runs a vetted network of 60+ technicians dispatched through the same real-time system — just faster, and with a lot more coffee.
