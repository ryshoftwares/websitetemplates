Our story

One point of accountability, by design.

ProServe was founded in 2013 as a two-person IT helpdesk contractor. Today it's a combined facilities-and-IT services firm built around a simple idea: businesses shouldn't need five vendors to keep the lights on and the network up.

How we started

Clients kept asking for one vendor, not five.

ProServe started in 2013 as a small IT helpdesk contractor serving a handful of Chicago-area law firms. As those clients opened additional office locations, they kept asking the same question: could we also manage the janitorial contract, the HVAC maintenance, the building access system — basically, could there be one vendor accountable for "the building working" instead of five separate contracts with five separate escalation paths?

In 2016 ProServe signed its first combined facilities-and-IT contract for a three-location retail client, building the operations playbook that still underlies every engagement today: one SLA, one invoice, one number to call. A 24/7 network operations center followed in 2019 to support growing IT demand from healthcare and financial clients.

ProServe now supports more than 140 client sites across five states from its Chicago headquarters, with a team of facilities technicians, helpdesk engineers and account managers who treat every site like it's their only one.

What we believe

Four principles behind every contract.

03

Straight Answers
