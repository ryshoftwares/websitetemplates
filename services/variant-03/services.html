How proposals work

Every quote starts with an audit, not a guess.

Why isn't there a flat price on this page?

Facilities and IT contracts vary too much by site count, square footage, coverage hours and existing infrastructure to quote sight-unseen. Our proposals are built from a short on-site or remote audit so the SLA actually reflects your risk, not a generic average.

Can we start with just IT or just facilities?

Yes — most clients start with one service line and add the other once they see how the reporting and SLA structure works in practice.

How long does onboarding take?

Typical transitions from an existing vendor take 2–4 weeks, run against a documented cutover plan so there's no coverage gap.

Ready to see what one contract would cost?

We'll scope your sites and send a proposal within a week.
