Pricing, explained

How we price a project.

Why is my quote a range on this page but fixed in my contract?

The ranges above reflect the spread of scopes and finish levels we build across dozens of similar projects. Once we've walked your space and you've chosen materials, we convert that range into one fixed number before you sign anything.

Do you require a deposit?

Most projects require a 10% deposit to schedule design and permitting, with the remaining balance billed against a milestone schedule tied to completed work — never in advance of it.

What if we find something unexpected once you open a wall?

We flag it immediately, explain the cost impact in writing, and get your sign-off before doing any additional work. Roughly 1 in 10 of our projects involves a documented change order — the rest finish at the quoted price.

Tell us what you're picturing and we'll help you scope it during a free in-home estimate.
