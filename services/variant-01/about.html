Our story

A carpenter's business, built one honest job at a time.

Anchor Services started in 2011 with one truck, one toolbox, and a simple idea: tell homeowners exactly what a project will cost and deliver exactly that.

How we started

From one carpenter to a 22-person crew.

Marcus Reyes spent eleven years framing houses and building custom cabinetry for other contractors before he founded Anchor Services in 2011. He'd seen too many homeowners get vague estimates, surprise change orders, and crews that disappeared halfway through a job — and he was convinced renovation didn't have to work that way.

He started by taking on small kitchen and bathroom jobs around Southeast Portland, doing the work himself and pricing every project down to the last fixture before signing a contract. Word spread. By 2015 Anchor had opened a small design showroom in the Sellwood neighborhood; by 2019 the crew had grown to fifteen people and completed its 100th project.

Today Anchor Services employs 22 carpenters, project managers and design consultants, and has completed more than 500 renovations across the Portland metro area — but the rule Marcus started with hasn't changed: the price you're quoted is the price you pay.
