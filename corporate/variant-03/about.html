About Sterling Partners

Two decades of advising companies that plan to still be here in twenty more.

Founded in Chicago in 2005, Sterling Partners has built its practice on long client relationships rather than one-off engagements — many of our clients have worked with the same partner for over a decade.

Our approach

Long relationships over quick wins.

We're not built for one-off engagements. Most of our clients return for a second and third project, working with the same partner each time — because continuity of understanding matters as much as the analysis itself.

Milestones

Twenty-one years of practice.

2005

Founded in Chicago by three partners from a national consulting firm, focused on mid-market strategy work.

2012

Added an operations advisory practice as clients increasingly needed help executing, not just planning.

2018

Built out a dedicated M&A advisory team to support clients through acquisitions and divestitures.

2026

18 partners and principals, with a 94% client retention rate.
