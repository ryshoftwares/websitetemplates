Our story

Founded by advisors who believed rigor and candor weren't mutually exclusive.

Meridian Group opened its doors in New York in 1994 with three partners and a conviction: that the best advice is independent, evidence-based, and said plainly — even when a client doesn't want to hear it. Today we're a 900-person partnership advising leadership teams across 40 countries.

Our mission

Advice you could defend in front of your own board.

We believe good advisory work has to survive scrutiny long after the engagement ends. Every project pairs senior partners directly with your leadership team — no army of first-year analysts fronted by a partner who appears twice. We measure ourselves by what changes after we leave, not by the thickness of the final report.

What we value

Four principles that shape every engagement.

Independence first

We hold no equity, sell no software and take no referral fees — our recommendation is the product.

Say the hard thing

If the honest answer is unwelcome, we say it anyway — clearly, and with evidence behind it.

Senior on every team

No engagement runs without a partner who has led this exact type of work before, more than once.

Built to be implemented

We design recommendations your own team can actually execute, not ones that only work on paper.

Milestones

Thirty-two years of building a different kind of advisory firm.

1994 — Founded in New York

Three partners left larger firms to start a practice built around independence and direct partner involvement.

2003 — Opened the London office

Grew past 80 consultants and opened a second office to serve European financial-services clients.

2014 — Launched our Operations & Performance practice

Built a dedicated team focused on cost transformation and supply chain resilience after the 2008 downturn reshaped client priorities.

2025 — 540+ engagements delivered

Crossed 540 completed engagements across financial services, manufacturing, retail, energy and healthcare.
