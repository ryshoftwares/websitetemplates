Our story

We started Forge because "it depends" isn't a strategy.

Founded in New York in 2017 by two former Big Four partners tired of writing recommendations designed never to be wrong, Forge & Co. exists to give leadership teams an actual answer — and stick around to help them execute it.

How we're different

A specific answer, not a menu of options.

Most consultancies hand you three scenarios and let you pick — so if it goes wrong, it was your call. We do the work to pick one, put our name on it, and stay through implementation to prove we were right.

What we stand for

Four rules we don't break.

Senior on every team
