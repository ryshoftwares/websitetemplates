Welcome to Solara

A coconut farm turned color-drenched hideaway.

Solara opened in 2016 on what used to be a family coconut farm along Tulum's coastal road. We kept the palms, added raw concrete and terrazzo, and asked local muralists to paint every casita a different mood. Breakfast starts at sunrise. The rooftop bar closes whenever the last person leaves.

Amenities

Everything a bold stay needs.

Beach Club & Pool

A freeform pool, striped cabanas, and direct beach access with a bar that mixes serious mezcal.

Cenote Spa

Treatments inspired by the region's limestone cenotes, with a cold-plunge pool and steam room.

Fuego Rooftop

Wood-fired tacos, ceviche and a mezcal list twelve bottles deep, served under string lights.

Concierge & Adventures

Sunrise yoga, snorkeling at the reef, jungle bike tours and cenote day trips, booked at the front desk.
