Amenities

Everything a bold stay needs.

Beach Club & Pool

A freeform saltwater pool, striped cabanas, and direct beach access, with a swim-up bar pouring mezcal cocktails from noon.

Cenote Spa

Four treatment rooms inspired by the region's limestone cenotes, a cold-plunge pool, and a jungle-facing steam room.

Fuego Rooftop

Chef Mariana Cetina's wood-fired tacos and ceviche menu, plus a mezcal list twelve bottles deep, served under string lights.

Concierge & Adventures

Sunrise yoga on the beach, reef snorkeling trips, jungle bike tours and cenote day trips, arranged at the front desk.
