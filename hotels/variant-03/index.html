Welcome to The Stay

Quiet is not the absence of anything.

The Stay opened in 2019 inside a decommissioned grain warehouse on Sundkrogsgade, on the harbour edge of Nordhavn. We kept the steel columns, the loading doors, and the height of the ceilings, and added almost nothing else. Rooms are unusually plain on purpose — good light, a good bed, and a desk you'd actually want to sit at.

Amenities

Everything a quiet stay needs.

Kornet

A quiet dining room and bar built into the original grain silos, serving modern Danish food.

The Bathhouse

A wood-fired sauna, cold plunge and a small gym, open early for guests who run before meetings.

The Loading Bay

A quiet workspace and two meeting rooms, named for the dock this building once loaded grain from.

Concierge

Bike rental, restaurant bookings and local guides, arranged at the front desk or before arrival.
