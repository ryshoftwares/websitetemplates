Amenities

Everything a quiet stay needs.

Kornet — Restaurant & Bar

A dining room and bar built into the original grain silos, serving a short, seasonal Danish menu and a bar list built around Nordic distillers.

The Bathhouse

A wood-fired sauna, a cold plunge pool, and a small gym with free weights and two rowing machines. Open from 6am.

The Loading Bay

A quiet, plug-equipped workspace and two small meeting rooms, named for the dock this building once loaded grain from.

Concierge & Bike Rental

Restaurant bookings, local guides and bicycles arranged at the front desk, 24 hours a day, or before you arrive.
