Welcome to Aurelia

Where the Amalfi Coast slows down.

Aurelia opened in 1974 in a converted lemon-terrace villa above Praiano, and has stayed family-run ever since. Every room faces the water. Breakfast is served until noon. Nothing here is rushed.
