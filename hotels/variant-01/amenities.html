Amenities

Everything a slow stay needs.

Private Beach Club

Reserved sun loungers, cabana service and a bar, reached by a five-minute cliffside path or our private funicular.

Grotto Spa

Six treatment rooms carved into the rock, a sea-water thermal pool, and a sauna overlooking the coastline.

Terrazza Restaurant

Chef Elena Marchetti's coastal Italian menu, built around the morning catch, on a terrace above the water.

Concierge & Transfers

Private boat charters, Path of the Gods hiking guides, and airport transfers from Naples, arranged on request.
