About me

Nine years of backend work, four of them freelance.

I started as employee number four at a logistics startup, where I learned the hard way what happens when a database schema doesn't scale. Since 2022 I've worked independently, joining startups for focused engagements rather than permanent roles.

How I work

Short engagements, permanent code.

I don't take on retainer work that never ends. Every engagement has a defined scope and a handoff plan — documentation, tests, and a walkthrough with your team, so the system I build keeps working long after I'm gone.
