My story

I learned to see light before I learned to use a camera.

I grew up in a house full of film cameras that never quite worked right, which meant I spent more time understanding light than pressing shutters. That habit never left.

Where it started

From studio assistant to independent photographer.

I started assisting a portrait studio in Seattle in 2014, learning lighting setups one wedding and one corporate headshot at a time. In 2016 I went independent and moved to Portland, where the overcast light turned out to be exactly what my portrait work needed. Eleven years on, I split my time between editorial portrait sittings, brand campaign travel, and the documentary weddings that first taught me to shoot without directing.

What I believe

Four principles that shape every shoot.

Light first

I choose the time and place before I choose the outfit or the pose. Everything else follows the light.

Unretouched honesty

I edit for color and tone, not for erasing the person in front of the lens.

Collaborative direction

The best portraits happen when the subject is in on the decision-making, not just posing for it.

Fast, clean delivery

A gallery within two weeks, culled and color-graded — no six-month wait for your own images.

Milestones
