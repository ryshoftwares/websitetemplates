About me

I got tired of designing the safe option.

I'm Joon Park — an illustrator and motion designer based in Los Angeles. I spent four years at an agency designing the "safe" version of every pitch. In 2018 I went out on my own so I could design the other one instead.

How I work

Loud, but never lazy.

Bold doesn't mean careless. Every illustration and identity system I ship goes through the same rigor a "safe" studio would apply — it just doesn't come out looking safe on the other end. I test ideas with real audiences before I commit a client's brand to them.

Timeline

Eight years, no boring clients.

2018

Left the agency world to freelance full-time, starting with a single illustrated album cover.

2020

Picked up motion design and started animating my own illustration work for clients.

2023

Landed my first full brand identity project, and haven't stopped taking them since.

2026

80+ projects in, still turning down the safe version of every brief.
