Our story

Good design should disappear into clarity.

Studio Clean was founded in Copenhagen in 2015 on a simple belief: the best design work doesn't announce itself — it just makes everything else make sense. Eleven years later, we're an eleven-person studio doing that for wellness, architecture and professional-services clients across Europe.

Our philosophy

Restraint is a design decision, not a limitation.

Founder Sofie Lund started Studio Clean on the conviction that a brand system doesn't need to shout to be remembered — it needs to be structured well enough that it holds together everywhere it appears. We favor grid discipline, a single confident accent color per brand, and typography that holds its nerve, over trend-chasing. Every system we hand over is documented clearly enough that a client's own team can apply it themselves, long after our part is done.

What we value

Four principles behind every engagement.

Clarity over decoration

We remove until only what's necessary remains — for us, and for the people who meet the brand every day.

Grid discipline

Every system we build rests on a structured grid; consistency is what makes a brand recognizable at a glance.

One confident accent

We choose a single accent color per brand system and use it with intention, not everywhere at once.

Systems, not one-offs

We hand over documented systems a client's team can apply consistently, long after the project ends.

Milestones

Eleven years of building quietly.

2015 — Founded in Copenhagen

Sofie Lund started Studio Clean as a one-person studio, taking on identity work from a rented desk in Copenhagen.

2018 — First international client

An architecture practice abroad commissioned our first cross-border identity system, and word began to travel.

2021 — A full-time team of eight

Demand for wayfinding and packaging work grew the studio into a full-time team of eight designers.

2025 — Eleven people, full digital capability

We reached a team of eleven and expanded into full digital-design engagements alongside our identity work.
