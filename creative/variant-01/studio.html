Our story

Founded by an editor who was tired of "premium" meaning generic.

Atelier Noir began in a Brooklyn loft in 2013. Thirteen years later we're a 19-person studio with rooms in New York and Paris, still working the way a good magazine does: research, narrative, system, then application.

Our philosophy

A brand is a subject, not a deliverable.

Camille Rousseau founded Atelier Noir after years spent inside editorial magazines and luxury branding agencies, watching "premium" design flatten into the same six typefaces and the same beige palette. We start every engagement the way a magazine starts a feature — with research and a point of view — before a single mark is drawn. Typography comes first, decoration comes last, and every application is judged against the narrative it was built to carry.

What we value

Four principles that shape every engagement.

Research before design

No mark gets drawn until we understand the client's world as well as they do.

Typography is the system

We build identities from type and grid outward — colour and imagery follow, never lead.

Restraint over decoration

Every flourish has to earn its place. If it doesn't serve the story, it doesn't ship.

Senior hands only

The people who present the concept are the people who drew it, from first sketch to final file.

Milestones

Thirteen years of building identities that last.

2013 — Founded in a Brooklyn loft

Camille Rousseau opened the studio with two collaborators and a single client: a small perfumer who wanted packaging worth keeping.

2016 — First international client

A Parisian hôtel group commissioned our first cross-border identity, and our work started travelling further than we did.

2019 — Opened the Paris studio

Grew past a dozen people and opened a second studio to work closer to our European clients.

2024 — Named a top-10 boutique branding studio

Recognised by a leading design press outlet for our packaging and editorial identity work, alongside studios many times our size.
