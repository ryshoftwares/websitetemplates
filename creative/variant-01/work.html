Selected work

Eight brands, four disciplines, one point of view.

A sample of identity, packaging, editorial and digital projects for premium and lifestyle clients. Filter by discipline to see more of any one.

Packaging

Maison Verrier

A perfume bottle and box packaging system for a niche Parisian perfumer, built around a single sculptural glass form and a restrained, letterpress-inspired label.

Branding

Rue & Co.

A full identity for a Manhattan fashion boutique, pairing a hand-drawn monogram with a spare, oversized typographic system across signage and packaging.

Editorial

Noor Quarterly

A ground-up redesign of a literary and culture quarterly, with a new grid, a custom display typeface pairing and a masthead built to survive a decade of issues.

Branding

Cabane Hôtel Group

A brand identity for a boutique hotel group spanning eleven properties, flexible enough to read as one group while letting each hotel keep its own personality.

Packaging

Fleuret Chocolatier

A packaging system for an artisan chocolatier, using a modular box format and a hand-illustrated pattern that changes with each seasonal collection.

Editorial

Atlas Review

A visual identity and layout system for a culture review, built on a single flexible grid that flexes between photo essays, criticism and long-form reporting.

Digital

Belmont & Ash

An e-commerce site and brand identity for a fine tableware brand, translating the tactility of the product photography into a slow, editorial browsing experience.

Packaging

Ondine Skincare

A brand identity and packaging system for a skincare line, built around a calm, clinical colour palette and a typographic mark that scales down to a single ampoule.
