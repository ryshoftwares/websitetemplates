Our story

We left the safe pitch on someone else's desk.

Mika Voss founded WILDFORM in 2019 after years of watching the safe option win the pitch meeting while the better idea got quietly filed away. WILDFORM only takes clients who want the version other studios were too scared to show.

Our mission

The braver idea, built properly.

Bold isn't the same as careless. Every experimental identity, every piece of packaging that looks like it shouldn't work, is stress-tested against real production constraints before it ships. We're not chasing weird for its own sake — we're chasing the idea that makes people stop scrolling and start staring.

What we value

Four rules nobody talks us out of.

01

The scarier brief wins

If the client isn't a little nervous looking at the concept, we haven't pushed hard enough yet.

02

No moodboard theatre

We build the real thing fast instead of presenting a hundred inspiration slides.

03

Craft under the noise

Loud only works when the typesetting, grid and production are flawless underneath it.

04

We say no a lot

Clients who want the safe version get a referral, not a watered-down version of us.

Milestones

Six years, out loud.

2019 — Founded above a Berlin record shop

Mika Voss started WILDFORM in a one-room studio above a record shop in Neukölln, with a single freelance client and a refusal to design another "safe" logo.

2021 — First UK client

An independent record label took a chance on the studio's most aggressive pitch yet — and it became the project that put WILDFORM on the UK music scene's radar.

2022 — London studio opens

Demand from UK fashion and culture clients pushed WILDFORM to open a second studio in London, running alongside the original Berlin space.

2025 — "The studio agencies are scared of"

An industry blog called WILDFORM out by name as the studio agencies are scared of losing pitches to. We printed it on a poster and hung it in reception.
