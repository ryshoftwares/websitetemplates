Your gift moves a family closer to move-in day.

Roughly 70 cents of every dollar goes directly to rental assistance and move-in costs. The rest funds the caseworkers, mentors and Neighbors Table gatherings that keep a family connected once they're housed.

70% — Rental & move-in assistance

Direct deposits, first month's rent and moving costs for families entering housing.

20% — Casework & mentorship

Salaries for caseworkers and coordinators who support New Keys mentors and families.

10% — Neighbors Table

Shared meals, pantry stock and community events that welcome families into their block.
