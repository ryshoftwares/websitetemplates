How we help

Housing is the start. Community makes it last.

Housing Assistance

Emergency rental assistance and move-in support that gets families off the street and into a lease, fast — through our Rapid Rehousing Initiative and Family Stability Fund.

Learn more

Mentorship

One-on-one mentorship pairs young adults aging out of foster care with a steady adult presence as they set up their very first independent home, through New Keys Mentorship.

Learn more

Community

Shared meals and neighbor-to-neighbor introductions through Neighbors Table help newly housed families feel like they belong on their block, not just that they have an address on it.

Featured story

The Alvarez family found stability in 42 days.

When Denise Alvarez lost her job and then her apartment, she and her two children spent six weeks moving between motels. Haven Trust's Rapid Rehousing Initiative covered her deposit and first two months of rent while a caseworker helped her line up new work. Fourteen months later, she's still in that apartment.
