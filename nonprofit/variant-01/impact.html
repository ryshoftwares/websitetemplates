Real stories

Two people, one program apart.

Beneficiary story

Maria Delgado

A single mother who spent three months in her car with her two children before a shelter connected her to Haven Trust. The Rapid Rehousing Initiative covered her deposit and helped her secure a one-bedroom apartment within five weeks.

“My kids finally have their own beds again. I never thought I'd cry over a lease, but I did.”

Volunteer story

James Whitfield

A retired contractor who has mentored four young adults through New Keys Mentorship since 2021, helping each one furnish their first apartment and stick to a budget through their first year of independence.

“I show up for two hours a week. What I get back — watching someone stand on their own — is worth a lot more than that.”

Numbers like these only move with your support.
