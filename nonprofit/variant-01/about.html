Our story

Founded by neighbors who were tired of watching families fall through the cracks.

Haven Trust started around a kitchen table in Portland in 2009. Seventeen years later we're a full housing and community-support organization working across the Pacific Northwest — but the kitchen-table instinct to know families by name hasn't changed.

Our mission

Stable housing, and the neighborhood to hold it up.

We believe housing is only half the answer. A family that gets a lease but no support system is still one bad week from losing it. Every Haven Trust program pairs direct housing assistance with a caseworker, a mentor, or a neighbor — so stability isn't something a family has to hold onto alone.

What we value

Four principles that shape every program.

Dignity first

Families choose their own path. We offer support, not conditions or judgment.

Neighbors, not numbers

Every household has a name, a story and a caseworker who actually knows both.

Stay the course

Support doesn't end at move-in. We stay engaged well past the first year of housing.

Open books

Donors and neighbors alike can see exactly how every dollar is put to work.

Milestones

Seventeen years of building, one household at a time.

2009 — Founded in Portland

Started with two caseworkers and a folding table at a local church, helping five families find emergency housing that first winter.

2014 — Launched the Family Stability Fund

Introduced short-term rental assistance and eviction-prevention casework to stop families from losing housing they already had.

2019 — Launched New Keys Mentorship

Built a dedicated mentorship program for young adults aging out of foster care into their first independent home.

2025 — Crossed 4,200 families housed

Reached a 38-partner network across the Pacific Northwest and a 92% twelve-month housing retention rate.
