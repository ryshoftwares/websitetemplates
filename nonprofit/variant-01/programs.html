Our programs

Four programs, one goal: a family that stays housed.

Each program meets a different moment — the crisis of losing housing, the risk of losing it, the leap into a first independent home, and the work of belonging once you're in it.

Program 01

Rapid Rehousing Initiative

Emergency rental assistance and move-in support for families facing homelessness right now. A dedicated caseworker helps identify an available unit, covers the deposit and initial rent, and stays engaged through the first months in a new home.

1,600+families moved in
38 daysaverage time to housing

Program 02

Family Stability Fund

Short-term rental assistance and eviction-prevention casework for households at risk of losing housing they already have — often after a medical bill, a lost job, or a rent increase. Serves households already leased across Multnomah, Washington and Clackamas counties.

2,100+evictions prevented
94%still housed one year later

Program 03

New Keys Mentorship

One-on-one life-skills mentorship for young adults aging out of foster care and into their first independent home. Mentors meet weekly for a year, covering everything from budgeting and job searches to simply having someone to call on a hard day.

310young adults mentored
89%housing-stable after year one

Program 04

Neighbors Table

A food-security and community-meals program that pairs newly housed families with their new neighborhoods. Monthly shared dinners, a rotating pantry, and neighbor-to-neighbor introductions turn an address into a community in the first ninety days.

900+households connected
38neighborhood partner sites

Every program runs on volunteers and donors.

Move-in day, mentorship, shared meals — pick where you'd like to help, and we'll take it from there.
