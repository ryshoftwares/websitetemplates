Our programs

Four programs, one pipeline from community to capitol.

Each program feeds the next — research becomes a briefing, a briefing becomes a bill, and a bill needs a coalition to pass.

Community Survey Research

Door-to-door and digital surveys that capture how a proposed policy will actually land in the neighborhoods it affects, before a single vote is cast.

Research

Legislative Briefings

Plain-language policy briefs delivered directly to legislators and agency staff, translating research findings into workable recommendations.

Advocacy

Advocacy Training

A six-week program that trains community members and local organizers to testify, meet with legislators, and advocate for themselves directly.

Training

Coalition Support

Operational and research support for coalitions of small nonprofits who don't have in-house policy staff of their own.
