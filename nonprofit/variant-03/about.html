About us

We started Bridge Foundation because good policy needs good data.

Founded in Washington, D.C. in 2011 by a small team of researchers and community organizers, Bridge Foundation exists to make sure the people affected by a policy are part of writing it.

Our belief

Nonpartisan doesn't mean neutral about the truth.

We don't take a side on every issue, but we do insist that policy decisions be made with real data from the communities they affect — not assumptions made on their behalf.

Our history

Fourteen years, three offices, one mission.

2011

Founded with a single research contract studying housing policy in Washington, D.C.

2015

Expanded our research methodology into a formal community-survey program used by four state legislatures.

2020

Opened a second office and grew our advocacy training program to include 30 partner organizations.

2026

Now informing policy work across nine states with a full-time staff of 22.
