How we work

Three things we do, in order.

Research

We gather the data policymakers don't have time to collect themselves — from the neighborhoods the policy will actually affect.

Brief

We put that research directly in front of legislators and agency staff, in language they can use.

Organize

We help the communities we study speak for themselves, not just be spoken about.
