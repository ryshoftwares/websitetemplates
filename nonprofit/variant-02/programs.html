Our programs

Four ways we put young people in the driver's seat.

Every RISE program pairs mentorship with something concrete — a paycheck, a safe place, a microphone. Here's how each one works.

Program 01

RISE Circles

Weekly peer mentorship circles that pair teens with a trained young-adult mentor who's walked a similar path. No lectures — just consistent, honest check-ins that build real trust over time.

Ages 13-17 · Weekly, in-person circles

Program 02

Launchpad Fellowship

A paid 10-week apprenticeship that teaches real job and entrepreneurship skills — resumes, interviews, budgeting, pitching — to mentees who are ready to build something of their own.

Ages 16-24 · Paid, 10-week cohort

Program 03

Streetlight Nights

A free evening drop-in space with meals, workshops and mentors on hand — four nights a week, for teens who just need somewhere safe to be until things at home settle down.

Ages 13-19 · Four nights a week, free

Program 04

Voices Forward

A storytelling and advocacy program that trains youth to speak at city council, on panels and in media about the issues that actually affect them — because policy made without them rarely works for them.

Ages 15-24 · Advocacy training + real testimony

Want to run a circle, mentor a fellow, or just show up?

Every program above runs on volunteer mentors and donor support. We'll find the right fit for you.
