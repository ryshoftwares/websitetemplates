Mentee story

Jaylen Brooks — from RISE Circles to Launchpad graduate.

Jaylen joined a RISE Circle at 15 after moving through three group homes. His mentor showed up every single Tuesday for two years — no exceptions. Jaylen went on to complete the Launchpad Fellowship and now works in IT support while mentoring his own circle.

Volunteer story

Aaliyah Cross — the mentor who became staff.

Aaliyah started as a Streetlight Nights volunteer, showing up two evenings a week around her day job. Five years later she coordinates every volunteer mentor across RISE's programs — proof that showing up consistently can turn into a calling.
