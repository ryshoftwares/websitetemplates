Our story

Started in a rec center. Powered by young people ever since.

In 2017, a handful of Atlanta youth workers got tired of watching good kids get written off by systems that never asked what they actually needed. So they asked. RISE Collective is the answer — still being written, by the young people living it.

Why we exist

Young people don't need saving. They need backup.

Too many programs treat young people like problems to manage instead of people with plans of their own. RISE flips that. We show up with mentors, paid opportunities and a microphone — then we get out of the way and let young people lead. Every circle, fellowship and campaign we run is built with the young people it serves, not just for them.

What we stand for

Four rules we don't break.

Young people lead

Every program has youth voice built into how it's designed, not just how it's marketed.

Show up consistently

One missed mentor session breaks trust that took months to build. We don't miss.

Pay for the work

Skill-building is labor. If it's worth teaching, it's worth a paycheck.

No savior complex

We're backup, not a rescue mission. Big difference in how we treat people.

Milestones

Nine years, out loud.

2017

Founded in a converted rec center in Southwest Atlanta with four volunteer mentors and twelve teens.

2019

Launched Streetlight Nights after mentees told us they needed somewhere safe on weeknights, not just weekly meetings.

2021

Piloted the paid Launchpad Fellowship with eight mentees and one small-business partner. Now runs three cohorts a year.

2026

3,000+ youth mentored, 22 partner schools, and a Voices Forward class that's testified at city council four times this year.
